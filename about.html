ABOUT CAREPOINT

Healthcare with a human touch.

A fictional pharmacy brand used to demonstrate responsive web design and front-end interactions.

Our approach

CarePoint's concept is to make everyday health and wellness information approachable, clear, and easy to navigate.

Product details, prices, services, pharmacy registration, address and contact details must be verified before any real commercial use.

Website features

Search, category filtering, price sorting and a browser-based demonstration cart.

Keyboard focus styles, semantic headings, live regions, accessible labels and reduced-motion support.

Page-specific titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, social metadata, sitemap and robots file.

Project values

💙 Clear information

✓ Accessible interactions

🤝 Respect for privacy

🔒 Secure-by-design planning
